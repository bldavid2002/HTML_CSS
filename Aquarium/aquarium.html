Rules

The game is played on a rectangular grid that is devided into blocks called Aquariums. You have to fill each Aquarium with water up to a certain level or leave it empty. The water level in each Aquarium is the same across its full width. The number outside the grid shows the number of filled cells horizontally and vertically.
